## Note on the Furniture Bits pack

The City Builder and Restaurant packs ship a `LICENSE.txt` inside their download, and both are
reproduced verbatim next to this file. The Furniture Bits download used for the two lamp models
did not include one. It is published by the same author, on the same distribution page
(`kaylousberg.itch.io`), under the same CC0 1.0 terms as its sibling packs.

This file records that provenance; it is a statement by this project, not a licence issued by
the author. Anyone redistributing these lamps should confirm the current terms at the source.

## Modifications

Models are used as downloaded. The scene applies its own scaling, rotation and material tinting
at runtime — no geometry or texture file has been edited.
